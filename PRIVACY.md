# Fabrix privacy policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

## What Fabrix collects

Nothing. The current beta runs on built-in sample data and sends no data off your device.

Fabrix has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. No data is sold or shared.

When a later build lets you connect your own Paperclip server, this policy will be updated before that build ships to describe exactly what is sent and stored.
