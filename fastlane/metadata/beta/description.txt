Fabrix is a native control room for Paperclip agent factories on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Try it two ways:
- Tap "Explore the demo" to see agents at work, pending approvals and live runs on built-in sample data.
- Connect your own self-hosted Paperclip server: enter its address and sign in with a Paperclip API key, OIDC, or both.

Please test connecting, reopening the app (it reconnects to your last factory), and "Forget Factory".
