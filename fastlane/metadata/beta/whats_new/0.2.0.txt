New in 0.2.0

- Connect your own Paperclip server: sign in with a Paperclip API key, single sign-on (OIDC), or both. Keys and tokens stay in the Keychain.
- Overview, task board, agents with pause and wake, org chart, approvals and activity now run on your factory's live data.
- Approve or reject with an 8-second undo. Decisions still waiting are sent before the app quits or goes to the background.
- Forget a factory to remove it and its saved keys.
- Dark mode.

Please test
- Connecting with a key or OIDC, then quitting and reopening (it reconnects to your last factory).
- Approving and rejecting, including Undo.
- What happens when the server is unreachable.

No account is needed: "Explore the demo" shows sample data.
