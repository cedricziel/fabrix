New in 0.3.0

- Sign in with your browser: approve Fabrix on your Paperclip server, no API key to copy. When a key is about to expire, Fabrix asks you to sign in again.
- Single sign-on reuses your browser's login, so an identity provider you're already signed in to takes one click.
- Switch companies and factories from the title menu. On Mac, the Factory menu has Cmd-1 to Cmd-9.
- Custom request headers under Advanced, for example a reverse proxy's access token.
- Decisions still in their undo window are sent before you switch factories, quit or remove one. If sending fails, the approval shows it, so check it after switching.
- Anonymous diagnostics help us find crashes and slow requests. Server names, addresses and tokens are hashed on your device; see the privacy policy.

Please test
- Signing in with the browser, cancelling it, and letting it expire.
- Switching between factories and companies with an approval still in its undo window.
- A factory behind a proxy that needs a custom header.

No account is needed: "Explore the demo" shows sample data.
